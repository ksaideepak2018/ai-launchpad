Headline: Want to scale your small business faster? Use AI where it moves the needle — not because it’s trendy.

Top AI tools to start with (by function):
• Marketing: Surfer SEO — speeds SEO/content impact with data-driven briefs (SMBs saw +150% organic traffic in cases).
• Sales: HubSpot CRM + Apollo — automates outreach and attribution so small teams close more deals without more headcount.
• Support: Tidio — affordable chat + AI replies that boost conversions (case studies report ~25% sales increase and 5x conversions).
• Ops/Automation: Zapier — no-code workflows that eliminate repetitive work (e.g., Premiere Property Group: $115K saved; teams reported hours saved at scale).
• Finance: QuickBooks + Float/Ramp — AI-assisted categorization and cashflow forecasting to reduce errors and improve runway.

Real SMB outcomes from research reports:
• Zapier — Premiere Property Group: $115K saved; other Zapier stories show significant hours saved and $134M booked revenue attributed to automations across customers.
• Surfer SEO — SMB case: ~150% increase in organic traffic after adopting AI-optimized content workflows.
• Tidio — SMB case: ~25% sales lift and up to 5x conversion improvements after deploying chat + AI responses.

30/60/90-day playbook (practical):
0–30 days: Pick one high-impact use case (support bot, invoice OCR, SEO content); appoint an AI steward + sponsor; baseline metrics and anonymize sample data; run an MVP with a vendor offering pre-built connectors.
31–60 days: Tune the model/workflow using real interactions; connect to CRM/ERP for attribution; A/B test AI vs human outputs; formalize DPA/security controls.
61–90 days: Automate monitoring, document SOPs, train staff, and expand to 1–2 new use cases; calculate payback and prepare case for scaling.

Quick tips: start narrow, measure time-saved and conversion lift, pseudonymize PII, require exportable data and clear SLAs.
